Frequently asked questions

The details matter.

Always rely on current, official plan documents and account terms—not assumptions based on a card design.

Are returns or daily ROI guaranteed?

No return should be treated as guaranteed unless a valid, enforceable agreement explicitly states the terms and the provider can substantiate the claim. Digital assets can be volatile, and users may lose some or all of their funds. Avoid relying on headline percentages alone.

How long do withdrawals take?

Withdrawal timing depends on the published policy, asset network, custody arrangements, compliance checks, and any lock-up or maturity conditions. Davault Defi should state its actual processing windows and fees here before launch; this template does not assume a timeline.

Can I exit before a plan matures?

Early withdrawal rules are plan-specific. The final page should clearly disclose whether early exits are allowed, whether penalties apply, and whether any principal or reward can be delayed or lost. Confirm these rules in the signed terms.

What security measures protect my assets?

Publish only verifiable security claims, including custody arrangements, access controls, key management, audits, incident reporting, and the limits of any protections. A dark-themed interface or security badge is not evidence that funds are risk-free.

Are weekly or monthly plan prices subscription fees?

This must be stated explicitly in the official plan documentation. Distinguish platform subscription charges from investment deposits, trading fees, network fees, and any other charges. The figures are left unfilled here because the reference prices could not be verified.
